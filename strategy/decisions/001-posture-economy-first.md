# 001 — Posture: economy first, opportunistic raider

- **status:** accepted
- **date:** 2026-09-11
- **decided by:** Commander

## Context
No victory condition; score = resources spent ÷ 1000 (`docs/game/mechanics.md` → Ranking). Universe Genesis runs 5× economy, so mines compound fast. We are rank-unknown (see `reports/status/`), 2 planets, no combat fleet, in the #1 player's alliance. Inactive players' stockpiles are collected by whoever gets there first.

## Options
1. **Economy first, opportunistic raids** — grow mines and planets; raid only inactives with a clean simulation. Pro: compounding, low variance, matches a bot that plays continuously. Con: leaves loot on the table when we lack fleet.
2. **Raider** — small mines, big fleet, live off others. Pro: fastest score early. Con: needs moons/phalanx to be safe, one lost fleet is a week of production, invites retaliation.
3. **Turtle** — defence and quests only. Pro: safe. Con: falls behind everyone who raids; defence does not compound.
4. **Quest completionist** — follow the 85 rungs. Pro: canonical tech order. Con: the ladder is an order, not a strategy; later rungs pay pocket money.

## Decision
Option 1, with the quest ladder as the research *order* and the Commander's words as the rule: "grow as big as possible, have defences, and take the opportunities from players that don't play actively before others do."

## Consequences
DOCTRINE posture line; raid rule needs a clean simulation; defence rule (006) exists; espionage exists to find inactives (005).

## Revisit when
- Our fleet can carry ≥ 500k per sortie and inactive targets in range exceed what we can collect.
- We are raided successfully twice in a week (posture too soft).
