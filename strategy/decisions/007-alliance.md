# 007 — Alliance: stay in BJACK as an umbrella

- **status:** accepted
- **date:** 2026-09-11
- **decided by:** Commander

## Context
Joined BJACK for the `not_alone` rung. It is the #1 player's alliance. Members cannot attack members; non-members can. ACS and allied defence exist for members (`docs/game/mechanics.md` → Joint fleets).

## Options
1. Stay, be quiet and useful, use the umbrella; coordinate before hitting actives.
2. Leave, avoid being a visible target of rival alliances.
3. Found our own.

## Decision
Option 1. Being under the strongest umbrella while small is worth more than the visibility costs; rival alliances can attack us either way. Full posture in `strategy/ALLIANCE.md`.
