# 005 — Espionage: neighbourhood watch

- **status:** accepted
- **date:** 2026-09-11
- **decided by:** Commander

## Context
Raid yield = loot ÷ round trip. Same galaxy distance is `2700 + 95·Δsystem`, so a target 40 systems out is ~2.5× the flight of one 6 systems out. Previous sessions scanned 79 systems and raided none of them profitably. Probes cost crystal; `galaxy` views cost 10 deut per jump; each scan is a fleet slot for its flight.

## Options
1. Neighbourhood watch: 5:310–322 every 12 h; targets with verdict `farm` rescanned before each sortie.
2. Wide sweep (70+ systems).
3. On demand only.

## Decision
Option 1. Intel we cannot act on within an hour's flight is not intel, it is a log.

## Revisit when
- Fleet slots ≥ 8 (computer 7) and cargo capacity ≥ 200k per sortie.
- The neighbourhood has no `farm` targets for a week.
