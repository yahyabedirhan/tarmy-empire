# 006 — Defence floor

- **status:** accepted
- **date:** 2026-09-11
- **decided by:** Commander

## Context
Loot is at most half of each pool; a raider's profit is loot minus losses. Defences do not leave debris and 70 % rebuild. Alliance membership stops BJACK members only. The ladder unlocks: light lasers (now), small shield dome (shielding 2), gauss (energy 6, shipyard 6), plasma later.

## Options
1. Floor tied to stockpile: defence value ≥ typical resting resources ÷ 4, plus the dome; keep stockpiles low by spending.
2. Minimal, rely on the umbrella.
3. Heavy turtle.

## Decision
Option 1. The cheapest defence is an empty warehouse; the second cheapest is enough turrets that the raider's simulation goes red. Ladder rungs (10 light lasers, small dome, 2 gauss) are the schedule.

## Revisit when
- We are hit and the report shows the defence did not change the raider's outcome.
- Stockpiles routinely exceed 1M (floor becomes expensive → reconsider fleet-save routines instead).
